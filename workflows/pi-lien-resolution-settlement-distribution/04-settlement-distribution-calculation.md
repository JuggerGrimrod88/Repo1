# Step 4: Settlement Distribution Calculation

## Internal use

Do not calculate this until every lien in the Step 1/2 table has a **final
written payoff figure**, not a claimed amount. A distribution built on a
claimed amount and later reduced can create a shortfall the firm has to
cover; a distribution built on a claimed amount that is later increased can
create a trust account deficiency. Hold back a reserve for any lien still
unresolved rather than guessing at its final number.

|---|---|---|

### Fee agreement check

- [ ] Contingency percentage in line B matches the signed fee agreement on
      file.
- [ ] If any jurisdiction-specific fee cap applies (e.g., a reduced
      percentage on the portion of recovery attributable to a minor, or a
      statutory cap in a medical malpractice component of the case),
      confirm it is applied correctly before finalizing line B.
- [ ] If the case involves a minor or incapacitated client, confirm
      whether court approval of the settlement and fee is required before
      distribution.

### Trust accounting checks before disbursement

- [ ] Settlement funds have cleared and are confirmed in the firm's trust
      account.
- [ ] Every figure in the lien payoff itemization is the final written
      number, not an estimate. No row in that table should still read
      "claimed."
- [ ] Sum of lines B + C + E + F reconciles exactly to line A.
- [ ] Disbursement checks or wires prepared for: attorney fee (to
      operating account), each lienholder individually, and the client.
- [ ] Two-person sign-off on the disbursement (preparer and reviewing
      attorney) before any check is released.
