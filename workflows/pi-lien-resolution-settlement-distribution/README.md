# PI Lien Resolution and Settlement Distribution Workflow

Five-step workflow for identifying liens, clearing them, and distributing a
personal injury settlement. Each step has an internal-use component (attorney
and paralegal working documents) and a client-communication component (plain
language, no jargon). Use the steps in order. Do not disburse funds until
Step 4 is reconciled against every lienholder's final written payoff.

## Steps

| Step | File | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-lien-identification-summary.md](01-lien-identification-summary.md) | Identify every lien and subrogation interest from correspondence, EOBs, and medical records. Produce the lien summary table. |
| 2 | [02-lien-validity-analysis.md](02-lien-validity-analysis.md) | Analyze each lien for statutory basis, perfection, and reduction arguments. |
| 3 | [03-lien-negotiation-letters.md](03-lien-negotiation-letters.md) | Send a reduction demand to each lienholder, citing law and the net recovery available. |
| 4 | [04-settlement-distribution-calculation.md](04-settlement-distribution-calculation.md) | Calculate gross recovery, fees, costs, lien payoffs, and net to client. |
| 5 | [05-client-settlement-statement-closing-letter.md](05-client-settlement-statement-closing-letter.md) | Generate the client settlement statement and closing letter. |

## Working rules

- Every lien amount in the file is a **claimed** amount until the lienholder
  sends a final written payoff figure. Never disburse against a claimed
  amount.
- Medicare and Medicaid liens must be resolved through the applicable
  federal or state portal (BCRC/Conditional Payment process for Medicare;
  the state Medicaid agency or its contracted recovery vendor for Medicaid).
  A private-party negotiation letter to these payers only starts the
  administrative process; it does not substitute for it.
- Get the plan document or Summary Plan Description before you argue an
  ERISA reduction. The reduction argument you can make depends entirely on
  the plan's actual reimbursement language, not on general ERISA principles.
- Hold the disputed portion of any contested lien in trust. Never distribute
  a client's net recovery around an unresolved lien.
- Client gets the plain-language version at every step where one exists.
  The internal version stays in the file.
