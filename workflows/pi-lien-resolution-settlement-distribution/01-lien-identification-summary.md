# Step 1: Lien Identification and Summary Table

## Internal use

### Sources to review

Pull every lien and subrogation interest from these sources before you
consider the list complete:

- Client intake sheet (ask directly: Medicare, Medicaid, private insurance,
  workers' comp, veterans benefits, union health plan).
- All medical bills and Explanation of Benefits (EOB) statements. An EOB
  showing a payer name and a "subrogation" or "third party liability" note
  is a lien candidate even with no formal notice on file.
- Correspondence file: search for letters from Medicare's Benefits
  Coordination & Recovery Center (BCRC), state Medicaid agency or its
  recovery contractor (e.g., HMS, Conduent, Optum), private insurer
  subrogation departments, ERISA plan administrators or their recovery
  vendors (e.g., Rawlings, The Health Special Interest Group, ELMC), and
  workers' comp carriers or third-party administrators.
- Medical records face sheets and billing pages, which list the payer of
  record for each date of service.
- Client's health insurance card and Summary Plan Description (SPD) or
  Certificate of Coverage, if available. Request the SPD directly from the
  plan administrator if the client only has the card.
- Hospital lien filings with the county clerk, where the jurisdiction
  permits statutory hospital liens.

### Lien summary table

Build one row per lienholder. Do not net anything at this stage; capture
the raw claimed amount.

| Lienholder Name | Type | Claimed Amount | Basis / Reference # | Contact Name | Phone | Email / Fax | Mailing Address | Date of Last Correspondence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Medicare BCRC] | Medicare | $[amount] | Conditional Payment Letter, CPL ref # [___] | [contact] | [phone] | [email/fax] | [address] | [date] | Initial CPL received / Final demand pending |
| [State Medicaid Agency] | Medicaid | $[amount] | Recovery notice # [___] | [contact] | [phone] | [email/fax] | [address] | [date] | [status] |
| [Employer Health Plan, self-funded] | ERISA | $[amount] | Plan claim # [___] | [contact] | [phone] | [email/fax] | [address] | [date] | Awaiting SPD |
| [Private Health Insurer] | Private health | $[amount] | Subrogation file # [___] | [contact] | [phone] | [email/fax] | [address] | [date] | [status] |
| [Workers' Comp Carrier] | Workers' comp | $[amount] | Claim # [___] | [contact] | [phone] | [email/fax] | [address] | [date] | [status] |

Type field must be one of: Medicare, Medicare Advantage/Part D, Medicaid,
ERISA, private health, workers' comp, hospital statutory lien, VA/TRICARE,
other (specify). Medicare Advantage and Part D plans are not traditional
Medicare; log them under the plan's own name, not "Medicare" (see Step 2
for why the recovery process is different).

### Verification checklist before moving to Step 2

- [ ] Every payer listed on any EOB or bill in the file has a row, even if
      no lien letter has been received yet.
- [ ] Client confirmed no additional coverage exists (ask about a spouse's
      plan, a second job's plan, or a union plan the client may not think
      of as "insurance").
- [ ] Medicare Secondary Payer questionnaire completed with the client, and
      Medicare's Common Working File (CWF) status confirmed if a Medicare
      claim number exists.
- [ ] SPD or Certificate of Coverage requested in writing for every ERISA
      or private health lien where reduction will be sought.
- [ ] Claimed amount in the table matches the source document exactly; note
      the source document name next to each figure.

## Client communication

Subject: Who is claiming money back from your settlement

We identified the following organizations that paid for some of your
medical care and have a legal right to be paid back out of your
settlement. This is normal in every injury case. Each of these amounts
will be paid out of your settlement before you receive your share, so we
seek a reduction from every lienholder where the law allows one. Some
lienholders, such as Medicare or an employer health plan with strong
reimbursement rights, may not be reducible by much or at all. We will
tell you plainly which is which as each one is resolved.

| Who they are | What they paid for | Amount they are claiming |
|---|---|---|
| [Lienholder name, plain description] | [medical care description] | $[amount] |

We will send you an updated list as each of these gets resolved. You do
not need to contact any of these organizations yourself. Doing so on your
own can interfere with our ability to negotiate a reduction, so please
route any letters or calls you receive from them to our office.
