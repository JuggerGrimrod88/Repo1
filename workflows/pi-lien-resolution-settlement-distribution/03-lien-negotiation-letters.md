# Step 3: Lien Negotiation Letters

## Internal use

Send one letter per lienholder. Pull the reduction theory and target payoff
from the Step 2 matrix. Send by the channel that lienholder actually uses
(Medicare and most Medicaid recovery vendors require their own portal or
form in addition to, or instead of, a letter; treat the letter below as the
narrative attachment to that submission, not a replacement for it).

### Template: Medicare (attach to BCRC dispute or final demand response)

> [Firm Letterhead]
>
> [Date]
>
> Benefits Coordination & Recovery Center
> [Address from CPL]
>
> Re: [Client Name] — Medicare Beneficiary ID [___] — Case/CPL Reference
> [___] — Date of Injury [___]
>
> Dear Sir or Madam:
>
> This office represents [Client Name] in connection with a personal
> injury claim arising from an incident on [date]. The claim has settled
> for a gross amount of $[gross settlement]. We write to dispute and
> request reduction of the conditional payment amount identified in your
> [Conditional Payment Letter / Demand] dated [date], reference [___].
>
> First, the following charges identified in the itemized conditional
> payment summary are unrelated to the injury at issue and should be
> removed: [list disputed line items with basis, e.g., pre-existing
> condition, unrelated body part, treatment date outside the causally
> related period].
>
> Second, pursuant to 42 C.F.R. § 411.37, Medicare's recovery must be
> reduced for procurement costs. Attorney fees and costs in this matter
> total $[fees + costs] against a gross settlement of $[gross].
> [If the conditional payment amount, after removing disputed items, is
> less than the gross settlement, use the standard ratio formula under §
> 411.37(a)-(b):] The procurement cost ratio is [%]. Applying that ratio
> to the conditional payment amount of $[claimed amount, after removing
> disputed items] yields an adjusted reimbursement obligation of
> $[calculated amount].
> [If the conditional payment amount equals or exceeds the gross
> settlement, use the special rule under § 411.37(c) instead — do not
> apply the ratio to the conditional payment amount:] Medicare's recovery
> is limited to the gross settlement less total procurement costs:
> $[gross] − $[fees + costs] = $[calculated amount].
>
> Please confirm the revised final demand amount in writing. We are
> prepared to remit payment promptly upon receipt of that confirmation.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Attorney Name]

### Template: Medicaid (state agency or recovery contractor)

> [Firm Letterhead]
>
> [Date]
>
> [State Medicaid Agency / Recovery Contractor Name]
> [Address]
>
> Re: [Client Name] — Medicaid ID [___] — Recovery File [___]
>
> Dear Sir or Madam:
>
> This office represents [Client Name] in the above-referenced matter,
> which has resolved for a gross settlement of $[gross settlement]. Your
> office has asserted a recovery claim of $[claimed amount] for medical
> assistance paid on the client's behalf.
>
> Under Arkansas Department of Health & Human Services v. Ahlborn, 547
> U.S. 268 (2006), and Wos v. E.M.A., 568 U.S. 627 (2013), Medicaid's
> recovery is limited to the portion of this settlement that fairly
> represents compensation for medical expenses. This settlement resolved
> claims for [describe full range of damages: past and future medical
> expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, etc.]. Based on [describe
> allocation basis — verdict data from comparable cases, mediator's
> valuation, itemized damages model], medical expenses fairly represent
> $[allocated medical amount] of the total $[gross settlement] recovery,
> or [%].
>
> We request that the Department's recovery be limited to $[allocated
> medical amount], further reduced by the Department's proportional share
> of attorney fees and costs under [cite state statute, if applicable].
> Please provide a written itemization if you dispute this allocation, and
> advise of any applicable hardship waiver procedure.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Attorney Name]

### Template: ERISA self-funded plan

> [Firm Letterhead]
>
> [Date]
>
> [Plan Administrator / Recovery Vendor Name]
> [Address]
>
> Re: [Client Name] — Plan [___] — Claim File [___]
>
> Dear [Contact Name]:
>
> This office represents [Client Name], a participant in [Plan Name]. We
> have received your correspondence asserting a reimbursement claim of
> $[claimed amount] against the proceeds of a third-party settlement in
> the amount of $[gross settlement].
>
> We have requested and reviewed the governing plan document. [If plan is
> silent on common fund/make-whole:] The plan document does not contain
> language displacing the common fund or make-whole doctrines. Under US
> Airways, Inc. v. McCutchen, 569 U.S. 88 (2013), those default equitable
> principles therefore govern. The plan's recovery should be reduced by
> its proportional share of the cost of obtaining this recovery
> (attorney fees and costs total $[amount], or [%] of the gross
> settlement), and in any event may not exceed the amount by which the
> client has been made whole for the full extent of the loss.
>
> [If plan contains clear reimbursement language:] The plan document
> contains express reimbursement language at [cite section]. We do not
> dispute the plan's right to reimbursement under Sereboff v. Mid-Atlantic
> Medical Services, Inc., 547 U.S. 356 (2006), but note that the client's
> net recovery after fees, costs, and other liens will be $[net to
> client before this lien], and request the plan consider a reduction to
> [proposed reduced amount] in recognition of the cost of obtaining this
> recovery and the client's ongoing unreimbursed losses.
>
> Please respond in writing with the plan's position within 30 days.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Attorney Name]

### Tracking

Log the date each letter is sent and each response received back in the
Step 1 summary table's Status column. Escalate any lienholder who has not
responded within 30 days with a follow-up call, logged the same way.

## Client communication

Subject: Update — we have sent reduction requests to every lienholder

We have now sent a formal request to each organization on your lien list,
asking them to reduce what they are claiming. Each letter is tailored to
that organization and cites the specific law that supports a lower
payment. This step typically takes two to six weeks depending on how
quickly each organization responds. We will update your lien summary as
responses come in and let you know if any lienholder pushes back in a way
that affects your timeline.
