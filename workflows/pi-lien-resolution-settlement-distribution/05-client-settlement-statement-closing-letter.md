# Step 5: Client Settlement Statement and Closing Letter

## Internal use

Generate both documents directly from the reconciled Step 4 worksheet.
Every figure must match line for line; do not round or summarize amounts
differently than they appear in the disbursement worksheet. Both documents
go in the client's permanent file. Get the client's signature on the
settlement statement before releasing the client's check, confirming
acknowledgment of the breakdown.

### Settlement statement — required fields

- Client name and case caption/claim number.
- Date of settlement and date of statement.
- Gross settlement amount.
- Attorney fee, stated as both a percentage and a dollar amount, with a
  reference to the signed fee agreement.
- Case costs, itemized (pull directly from the Step 4 cost itemization
  table).
- Each lienholder, listed individually with: type, original claimed
  amount, final negotiated payoff, and reduction obtained.
- Net amount paid to client.
- Signature line for the client and the responsible attorney.

### Closing letter — required content

- Confirmation the matter is fully resolved and the file will be closed.
- Statement of where the settlement check and any disbursement records are
  being sent or have been sent.
- Plain-language note on any ongoing obligation of the client (e.g.,
  future Medicare set-aside obligations if applicable, tax treatment note
  directing the client to a tax professional, any remaining lien dispute
  not yet fully resolved that the client should be aware of).
- File retention/destruction policy notice per firm policy and state bar
  rules.
- Invitation to contact the office with questions, and a direct thank-you.
